WizCap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

WizCap ("the app") is developed by Ori Wiesel. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your videos stay on your device

Videos you open in WizCap, their audio, the transcripts and the captioned videos it creates are processed entirely on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Advertising

WizCap is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Information about your interactions with ads, and app diagnostics

How Google uses this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time from "Privacy & ad settings" in the app. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings.

Children

WizCap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows when it last changed.
